At the Algorithmic Systems Theory (AST) Group, we work at the intersection of systems and control theory, formal methods, and machine learning. Our research turns rigorous guarantees into efficient algorithms for autonomous systems and robotics. Led by Prof. Lars Lindemann, we are part of the Automatic Control Laboratory at ETH Zürich.
