We welcome inquiries from prospective students and collaborators who have a genuine interest in our work that aligns with those of the AST Group. We particularly appreciate concise, personalized emails rather than AI-generated messages.

For job or student project opportunity inquiries, include your curriculum vitae (including transcript of records for students), research interests, and intended timeframe.

Due to the high volume of inquiries, we unfortunately cannot respond to every email.
