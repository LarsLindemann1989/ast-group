title: Privacy
---

The site does not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or embedded social media. Publication search and filters run in your browser. There is no contact form; email links open your mail application. Images and fonts are served locally or supplied by your device.

## Hosting

GitHub Pages may process technical access data, including IP addresses, when you visit the site. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for information about GitHub’s data handling.

## External links

Links to services such as Google Scholar, Google Maps, publishers, and personal profiles open external websites with their own privacy policies.
