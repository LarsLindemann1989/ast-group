We study how autonomous agents can coordinate their actions and make decisions in environments shaped by other decision makers. These settings introduce coupled dynamics, shared objectives or competing priorities, and uncertainty about how others will respond.

Our research connects distributed control and formal specifications with planning under interaction. We seek methods that reason explicitly about dependencies between agents, including how one agent's policy changes another's behavior. This perspective links guarantees for individual systems to collective tasks and coordination across networks.

### Current directions

- Distributed control and coordination under logical task specifications.
- Planning with uncertain and responsive agents.
- Collective tasks, communication constraints, and coupled system dynamics.
